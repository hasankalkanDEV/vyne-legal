Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Vyne handles consumer health data, as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and Nevada's consumer health data law (SB 370). Vyne is made and run by one independent developer, Hasan Kalkan; "I" and "me" below mean him.

It covers the same data as the "Cycle tracking" section of the Vyne Privacy Policy, which applies too.

In short

  • It stays on your phone, or goes to the cloud only end-to-end encrypted.
  • I can't read the encrypted cloud copy.
  • Never sold, never shared, no third parties.
  • You can see, export and delete all of it in the app.

What consumer health data Vyne handles

Only your cycle tracking log, and only if you turn cycle tracking on (it is off until you do). That is what you enter or import:

  • temperatures
  • period and bleeding
  • cervical mucus and cervix observations
  • LH and pregnancy test results
  • symptoms and how you feel
  • sex
  • things that may have affected a reading
  • your own marks on the chart, and your notes

Vyne does not read Apple Health or Android Health Connect.

Why it is used

Only to show your own log back to you: your chart, your days, and the files you ask for (an export, or a PDF for a clinician). It is not used for advertising, analytics, research or to train any model.

Where it comes from

Only from you:

  • what you enter in the app;
  • files you choose to import: a CSV file exported from another tracking app, or a Vyne cycle file. The file is read on your phone, and the copy the file picker made is deleted right away.

Where it is kept

On your phone, in Vyne's own storage.

Vyne's cloud: only if you have turned on end-to-end encryption for your account. Your data is then encrypted on your phone, with a key that only your devices hold, before it leaves the phone, so I cannot read it and cannot hand it to anyone in readable form. Without end-to-end encryption, cycle data never leaves your phone through Vyne. If you turn end-to-end encryption off, the encrypted copies are deleted from Vyne's cloud and your data stays on your phones.

It is never placed in a shared profile.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include the app's local files, cycle data among them, depending on your device settings. Apple or Google run those backups under their own terms; Vyne does not control them.

Sharing

  • I do not sell your consumer health data, and I do not share it with anyone.
  • No third party receives it. Vyne has no advertising or analytics tools.
  • Crash reports (Sentry) are filtered to exclude it: while a cycle screen is open no activity trail is recorded, cycle terms are removed from every report, and a crash inside a cycle screen is reported as one fixed message without content.
  • An export or a PDF goes only where you send it yourself, through your phone's share sheet.

Your rights

  • See it: everything you logged is in the app.
  • Get a copy: Export (Vyne file or spreadsheet), or the PDF for a clinician.
  • Delete it: Settings → Cycle tracking → Delete all cycle data removes every day from your phone and from the encrypted cloud copy.
  • Delete your account: this deletes your cloud data and offers to delete cycle data from your phone too.
  • Stop: turning cycle tracking off stops it and keeps what you logged; delete it as above if you want it gone.
  • Anything else, or a question about your data: email vyneapp.help@gmail.com.

I answer requests within 45 days. If I need more time, I can extend this once by up to 45 more days, and I will tell you.

How to appeal

If I turn down your request, or you disagree with my answer, reply to the same email at vyneapp.help@gmail.com and say you are appealing. I will look at it again and write back with what I decided and why.

If your appeal is denied, you can contact your state Attorney General: people in Washington, the Washington State Attorney General; people in Nevada, the Nevada Attorney General.
