Vyne — Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Vyne's optional Cloud Backup keeps a copy of your data (through Google Firebase) so you can restore it on another phone. If you'd like to delete your account and that cloud data, here's how.

Option 1: Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Vyne and go to the More tab (bottom right).
  2. Scroll down to the Cloud Backup section.
  3. Tap "Delete My Account & Cloud Data" and confirm.

The button appears there once you are signed in to Cloud Backup. Deletion is immediate and permanent; there is no waiting period.

Option 2: Ask by email

If you can't use the app (you lost your phone, uninstalled it, and so on), email me at vyneapp.help@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask me to delete it. I'll write back once it's done.

What gets deleted

Your account (email and password sign-in) and everything in Cloud Backup — branches, habits, due dates, amounts, notes, lists, reminders, your journal and weekly diary, and the people you added — are permanently deleted from the servers, including backups in older formats. This can't be undone.

What's kept

Nothing is kept on the servers after deletion. Data on your phone isn't touched by deleting your cloud account: it stays there until you delete it in the app or uninstall Vyne. Crash reports in Sentry aren't linked to your account and are deleted automatically after a limited time.
