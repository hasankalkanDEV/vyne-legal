Vyne — Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Vyne's optional Cloud Backup lets you keep a copy of your data (via Firebase) so you can restore it on another device. If you'd like to delete your account and that cloud-stored data, here's how.

Option 1: Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Vyne and go to the More tab (bottom right).
  2. Scroll down to the Cloud Backup section.
  3. Tap "🗑️ Delete My Account & Cloud Data" and confirm.

The button appears there once you are signed in to Cloud Backup. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no waiting period.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app (lost your device, uninstalled it, and so on), email us at vyneapp.help@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm once it's done, usually within a few days.

What gets deleted

Your account (email and password sign-in) and everything stored in Cloud Backup — your categories, habits, notes, due dates and amounts — are permanently deleted from our servers. This can't be undone.

What's kept

Nothing is retained on our servers after deletion. If you also have data saved locally on a device (Vyne works fully offline too), deleting your cloud account doesn't touch it: it stays on your device until you delete it there or uninstall the app.

Contact
