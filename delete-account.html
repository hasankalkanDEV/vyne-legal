Vyne — Delete Your Account

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Vyne's optional Cloud Backup lets you keep a copy of your data (via Firebase) so you can restore it on another device. If you'd like to delete your account and that cloud-stored data, here's how.

Option 1: Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Vyne and long-press your avatar (top right) to open the profile menu.
  2. Scroll to the Cloud Backup section.
  3. Tap "🗑️ Delete My Account & Cloud Data" and confirm.

This is immediate and permanent — there's no waiting period.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app (lost your device, uninstalled it, etc.), email us at vyneapp.help@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm once it's done, usually within a few days.

What gets deleted

Your account (email/password sign-in) and everything stored in Cloud Backup, your categories, habits, notes, due dates, are permanently deleted from our servers. This can't be undone.

What's kept

Nothing is retained on our servers after deletion. If you also have data saved locally on a device (Vyne works fully offline too), deleting your cloud account doesn't touch that, it stays on your device until you delete it there or uninstall the app.

Contact

Questions about account deletion: vyneapp.help@gmail.com
