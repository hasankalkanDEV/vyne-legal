Vyne — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Vyne ("the App") is a personal app for habits, notes and a journal. It is made and run by one independent developer, not a company; "I" and "me" on this page mean that developer. This page explains what the App collects, why, and how you can control or delete it.

  • Everything stays on your phone unless you turn on Cloud Backup.
  • No account is needed, there are no ads, and your data is never sold.
  • Crash reports go to Sentry, without your name or email.
  • Cloud Backup is not end-to-end encrypted: I could technically read it, and I don't.

What Vyne collects

Vyne works fully offline and needs no account. Everything you create — branches, habits and their check-ins, due dates, amounts, notes, lists, reminders, your daily journal and weekly diary, the people you add and their birthdays, and your profile name — is stored on your device. Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on Cloud Backup or export a backup file yourself.

  • If you turn on Cloud Backup, the email address you sign up with is handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service), so you can sign back in and restore your data on another phone. Your password is handled by Firebase; I never see it.
  • Your content (everything listed above) is then also stored in Firebase Firestore (a Google Cloud database), linked to your account. Along with it, Vyne stores a random ID for each installation of the app and the platform (iOS or Android), so it can tell which phone wrote a backup. That ID is created by Vyne, isn't tied to your hardware or to any advertising ID, and changes if you reinstall.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics are sent to Sentry, as described in the next section.

Vyne does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or browsing activity, and never asks for your real name. People you add on the People page are typed in by you; Vyne does not read your phone's contacts. Vyne shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

Crash and performance diagnostics

Vyne uses Sentry, a crash-reporting service, to find and fix bugs. When the app runs into an error, Sentry receives technical details about it: the error message, the screen it happened on, the device model, OS version and app version, an anonymous identifier for the device, and a short trail of the most recent taps and screens before the problem. Vyne also sends Sentry basic performance timings, such as how long a screen took to load. This data is not linked to your name or email, it is used only to diagnose problems, and Sentry does not use it for advertising or for its own purposes. Sentry keeps it for a limited time and then deletes it automatically.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Vyne schedules them on your device, at the times you choose: reminders for due dates, list items and reminders; a habit's own reminder time; the birthdays of people you add; an optional daily check-in; and a short weekly note on Monday mornings. They are created on your phone and never involve sending your data to a server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Calendar

If you tap "Add to Calendar", Vyne opens your phone's own new-event screen with that item filled in, and you decide whether and where to save it. Vyne does not ask for calendar access and cannot read your calendar.

Backup files

Vyne can export your data as a backup file, and you choose which profiles go into it. The file is created on your device and handed to your phone's share sheet, so it goes wherever you send it; I never receive a copy. When you import a file, you choose "Add" (only what is missing is added) or "Replace" (a profile that is already on the phone is overwritten by the file's copy). Your last 3 imports can be undone, and profiles that aren't in the file are never touched.

How your data is protected

Cloud data is linked to your account, and the database's access rules stop other users from reading it. Google encrypts it in transit and at rest, but it is not end-to-end encrypted: as the operator of the database, I am technically able to access it. I don't look at your content, except when you ask me for help with your own data or when the law requires it. Cloud Backup data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States; if you use it from outside the US, your data is transferred there. On your phone, your data is protected by the operating system's app sandbox.

Sharing with third parties

I don't sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. The only third-party services involved are Google Firebase (sign-in and backup storage, on my behalf) and Sentry (crash and performance diagnostics). Neither uses your data for its own purposes. Apple and Google, as the app stores, may collect information about downloads and purchases under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

Cloud data is kept only as long as your account exists; there is no separate archive. You can permanently delete your account and cloud data any time from the More tab → Cloud Backup → "Delete My Account & Cloud Data" (the button appears once you are signed in). It takes effect immediately. You can also email me and I'll delete it for you. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your phone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, Turkey's KVKK, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. You can do most of this yourself in the app: edit anything, export a backup file, or delete your account. For anything else, email me.

Children

Vyne is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created a Cloud Backup account, email me and I'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
