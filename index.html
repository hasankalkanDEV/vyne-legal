Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Vyne ("the App") is a personal app for habits, notes and a journal. It is made and run by one independent developer, not a company; "I" and "me" on this page mean that developer. This page explains what the App collects, why, and how you can control or delete it.

In short

  • Everything stays on your phone unless you turn on Cloud Backup.
  • No account is needed, there are no ads, and your data is never sold.
  • Crash reports go to Sentry, without your name or email.
  • Cloud Backup is not end-to-end encrypted, except cycle data when you turn on end-to-end encryption: I could technically read the rest, and I don't.

What Vyne collects

Vyne works fully offline and needs no account. Everything you create is stored on your device:

  • Branches, habits and their check-ins, due dates and amounts
  • Notes, lists and reminders
  • Your daily journal and weekly diary
  • The people you add and their birthdays
  • Your profile name

Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on Cloud Backup or export a backup file yourself.

  • If you turn on Cloud Backup, the email address you sign up with is handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service), so you can sign back in and restore your data on another phone. Your password is handled by Firebase; I never see it.
  • Your content (everything listed above) is then also stored in Firebase Firestore (a Google Cloud database), linked to your account. Along with it, Vyne stores a random ID for each installation of the app and the platform (iOS or Android), so it can tell which phone wrote a backup. That ID is created by Vyne, isn't tied to your hardware or to any advertising ID, and changes if you reinstall.
  • If you share a profile, its content and the member details described under "Shared profiles" are stored in a shared space that the other member can see.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics are sent to Sentry, as described in the next section.

Vyne does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or browsing activity, and never asks for your real name. People you add on the People page are typed in by you; Vyne does not read your phone's contacts. Vyne shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

Crash and performance diagnostics

Vyne uses Sentry, a crash-reporting service, to find and fix bugs. When the app runs into an error, Sentry receives:

  • the error message and the screen it happened on
  • the device model, OS version and app version
  • an anonymous identifier for the device
  • a short trail of the most recent taps and screens before the problem

Vyne also sends Sentry basic performance timings, such as how long a screen took to load. This data is not linked to your name or email, it is used only to diagnose problems, and Sentry does not use it for advertising or for its own purposes. Sentry stores it on servers in the United States, keeps it for a limited time and then deletes it automatically.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Vyne schedules them on your device, at the times you choose:

  • reminders for due dates, list items and reminders
  • a habit's own reminder time
  • the birthdays of people you add
  • an optional daily check-in
  • a short weekly note on Monday mornings

They are created on your phone and never involve sending your data to a server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Calendar

If you tap "Add to Calendar", Vyne opens your phone's own new-event screen with that item filled in, and you decide whether and where to save it. Vyne does not ask for calendar access and cannot read your calendar.

Backup files

Vyne can export your data as a backup file, and you choose which profiles go into it. The file is created on your device and handed to your phone's share sheet, so it goes wherever you send it; I never receive a copy. When you import a file, you choose:

  • "Add": only what is missing is added.
  • "Replace": a profile that is already on the phone is overwritten by the file's copy.

Your last 3 imports can be undone, and profiles that aren't in the file are never touched.

Cycle tracking

Cycle tracking is off until you turn it on. When you turn it on, Vyne keeps the health data you enter or import: temperatures, period and bleeding, cervical mucus, cervix observations, LH and pregnancy test results, symptoms, how you feel, sex, things that may have affected a reading, your own marks on the chart, and your notes.

Where it is kept. On your phone, in Vyne's own storage. Like all app data, it is also part of your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), which Apple or Google run under their terms, not ours.

Vyne's cloud. Cycle data reaches Vyne's cloud only if you have turned on end-to-end encryption for your account. It is then encrypted on your phone with a key that only your devices hold, before it leaves the phone. We cannot read it, and we cannot hand it to anyone in readable form. Without end-to-end encryption, cycle data stays on your phone and is not sent to Vyne's cloud. If you turn end-to-end encryption off, the encrypted cycle copies are deleted from Vyne's cloud and your data stays on your phones.

Never shared. We do not share, sell or rent cycle data. It is never placed in a shared profile. It is not used for advertising, analytics, research or to train any model, and Vyne has no advertising or analytics tools.

Files you make. Import reads a file you choose on your phone. Export (Vyne file, spreadsheet) and the PDF for a clinician are made only when you ask, and go only where you send them through your phone's share sheet.

Error reports. Vyne sends crash reports to Sentry to fix bugs. While a cycle screen is open no activity trail is recorded, cycle terms are removed from every report, and a crash inside a cycle screen is reported as one fixed message without content.

Deleting it. Settings → Cycle tracking → Delete all cycle data removes every day from your phone and from the encrypted cloud copy. Deleting your account deletes your cloud data and offers to delete cycle data from your phone too. Uninstalling Vyne removes the data from the phone (a copy may remain in your phone's own backup until that backup is replaced).

Not medical advice. Vyne shows what you logged. It does not predict, does not tell you when you are fertile or safe, is not medical advice and is not birth control.

Consumer health data (for example Washington's My Health My Data Act). The categories of consumer health data are listed above; we collect them only to show them back to you; they come only from you (what you enter or import); we share them with no one. You can see all of it in the app, export it, and delete it at any time as described above; to ask us anything about it, write to vyneapp.help@gmail.com.

Shared profiles

You can share one of your profiles with one other person. This needs Cloud Backup to be on, and it only starts when you send an invite and they accept it.

While a profile is shared, everything in it (branches, habits and their history, notes, lists, journal, calendar items and time clock entries) is stored in a shared space in the same Firebase database as Cloud Backup. Both of you can see, change and delete everything in it, including what was in the profile before the other person joined. Members are equal: neither of you can remove the other. Your other profiles, notes and settings stay in your private backup and are not shared.

About its members, a shared profile stores your account ID, the date you joined, and the name you chose to show to the other member (it starts as your profile name; you can change it or leave it empty, and then you appear as "Other member"). For each habit day, it stores which member marked it and when; the other member sees this as "Done by". Like everything Vyne syncs, each item also carries when it was last changed and the random installation ID of the phone that changed it. The other member does not see your email address. Vyne does not record when you open the app, what you look at, or where you are.

An invite is a code and a link that you send yourself, through the app you choose (for example Messages). It includes the profile's name, works once and expires after 24 hours. The invite record (the code, the profile it is for, your account ID and, once used, the account ID of the person who joined) is kept until it is cancelled or replaced, or until you leave the profile or delete your account.

The other member can see everything in the shared profile. Like Cloud Backup, it is not end-to-end encrypted: as the operator of the database I am technically able to access it, and Google stores it on my behalf. Anything the other member can see, they can also keep: for example by keeping a private copy when they leave, by exporting a backup file, or with a screenshot. Copies like these are outside my control, so only share a profile with someone you trust.

When a branch, note, list, calendar item or time clock entry is removed from a shared profile, a copy goes to "Recently removed", where either member can restore it for 30 days. After that it is deleted the next time either of you opens Vyne, so it can stay longer if neither of you opens the app. Entries inside a list, log lines and single ticks are not kept there (they have the usual Undo), and neither is an item moved to a private profile.

Leaving a shared profile

You can leave a shared profile at any time (Shared space screen → "Leave this shared profile"), and you can choose to keep a private copy of it. The copy goes into your private backup, without the record of who did what. When you leave:

  • the shared profile is removed from your phones;
  • your name, account ID and join date are removed from its member list, and your account ID is replaced by a random ID in its items and in Recently removed; the shared profile keeps that random ID and the date you left;
  • invites you made for it, and the one you used to join, are deleted;
  • the other member keeps the shared profile and everything in it, including what you added, and sees "Former member" where your name was.

This does not change text anyone typed into the profile: if you wrote your name or other details into a note, they stay there until someone edits or deletes them. If the other member's phone is offline, it can keep your name and account ID in its own copy until it next connects; then it removes them, keeping only a scrambled, one-way form of your account ID, used only to make sure your ID is never written back. If you are the last member, leaving deletes the shared profile and everything in it, including Recently removed, from the servers.

If you delete your account, Vyne first takes you out of every shared profile as described above, without keeping a private copy, and removes them from your phone. A shared profile with no other member is deleted with all its content, and your invites are deleted. Only after that are your private backup and your sign-in deleted. If Vyne cannot finish leaving a shared profile (for example, you are offline), nothing in your private backup or account is deleted and you can try again; shared profiles you have already left stay left.

After you leave or delete your account, what you added to a profile that another member still uses stays with them, because it is their profile too, without your name or account ID. If you want something you added gone, delete it from the shared profile first; it then stays in Recently removed for 30 days, where the other member can still restore it, and is deleted after that.

How your data is protected

Cloud data is linked to your account, and the database's access rules stop other users from reading it, except the other member of a profile you chose to share. Google encrypts it in transit and at rest, but it is not end-to-end encrypted (the exception is cycle data once you turn on end-to-end encryption, as described under "Cycle tracking"): as the operator of the database, I am technically able to access it. I don't look at your content, except when you ask me for help with your own data or when the law requires it.

Cloud Backup and shared profile data (Google Firebase) and crash reports (Sentry) are stored on servers in the United States; if you use Vyne from outside the US, this data is transferred there. On your phone, your data is protected by the operating system's app sandbox.

Sharing with third parties

I don't sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. The only third-party services involved are Google Firebase (sign-in and backup storage, on my behalf) and Sentry (crash and performance diagnostics). Neither uses your data for its own purposes. Apple and Google, as the app stores, may collect information about downloads and purchases under their own privacy policies. If you share a profile, its content is also visible to the person you invited (or who invited you), as described under "Shared profiles".

Why I can use your data

The person responsible for your data (the "data controller") is Hasan Kalkan, the independent developer of Vyne, reachable at vyneapp.help@gmail.com. I use your data only on these grounds:

  • Cloud Backup: to give you the service you asked for, keeping your data and returning it to you (performance of a contract; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Shared profiles: to give you the sharing you asked for (performance of a contract, as above). When you leave or delete your account, what you added stays for the other member, with your name and account ID taken out: their and my legitimate interest in keeping their profile whole (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Crash and performance data: my legitimate interest in keeping the app working and fixing bugs (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). You can object by emailing me.
  • Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or automated decisions about you.

Keeping and deleting your data

Cloud data is kept only as long as your account exists; there is no separate archive. The exception is a shared profile: what you added stays with the other member after you leave or delete your account, as described under "Leaving a shared profile". You can permanently delete your account and cloud data any time from the More tab → Cloud Backup → "Delete My Account & Cloud Data" (the button appears once you are signed in). It takes effect immediately. You can also email me and I'll delete it for you, taking the same steps for shared profiles. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your phone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, Turkey's KVKK, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. You can do most of this yourself in the app: edit anything, export a backup file, or delete your account. For anything else, email me.

Information notice for Turkey (KVKK)

Together with the sections above, this is the information notice under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). The data controller, the data, the purposes and legal grounds, and who receives the data are described above, in "What Vyne collects", "Shared profiles", "Sharing with third parties" and "Why I can use your data".

  • How it is collected: electronically, through the app, when you sign up, when you use Cloud Backup or sharing, and automatically when the app runs into an error.
  • Transfer abroad: Cloud Backup and shared profile data are stored by Google, and crash reports by Sentry, on servers in the United States. Cloud data goes there only if you turn on Cloud Backup, and for that I rely on your explicit consent, which you give by turning it on; you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account. Crash reports are not linked to your name or email.
  • Your rights under KVKK Art. 11: to learn whether your data is processed and, if so, to ask for information about it; to learn the purpose and whether it is used for that purpose; to know the third parties in Turkey or abroad it is transferred to; to ask for incomplete or wrong data to be corrected, and for it to be deleted; to ask that a correction or deletion be passed on to those third parties; to object to an outcome against you that arises only from automated analysis; and to ask for compensation if unlawful processing causes you damage.

To use these rights, email me at vyneapp.help@gmail.com from your account's email address. I answer free of charge within 30 days at the latest.

Children

Vyne is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created a Cloud Backup account, email me and I'll delete it.

Changes to this policy
